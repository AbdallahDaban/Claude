# Fleetana launch social plan: Instagram (first 9) and LinkedIn (first 3)

Everything is in light mode, with no pricing. Every asset exists in Arabic (`social/ar/`) and English (`social/en/`).

**Recommendation:**
- Instagram in Arabic visuals, with the caption in Arabic first and English below it.
- LinkedIn in English visuals, with the post in English first and Arabic below it.

The other-language files are ready if you want to switch either channel.

Ground rules are the same as the films:
- Every claim maps to something your code ships.
- There are no invented numbers.
- Every GPS-dependent feature carries "Requires a GPS tracker".
- No emoji and no exclamation marks, per the brand voice.

## How the Instagram grid will look

Instagram shows the newest post top-left. After all 9 are posted, your profile reads like this:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **9** Driver scores (image) | **8** Spreadsheet vs Fleetana (carousel) | **7** Live map (reel) |
| **6** Built for Saudi fleets (image) | **5** How the fuel flag works (carousel) | **4** Where the money goes (reel) |
| **3** Istimara, listed 30 days ahead (image) | **2** Your whole fleet, one place (carousel) | **1** Spreadsheets & WhatsApp (reel) |

The columns line up: reels on the right, carousels in the middle, single images on the left. Backgrounds alternate white and light blue in a checkerboard. Preview: `social/ar/instagram-grid-preview.png` (and `social/en/…`). All post text sits inside the 3:4 area the profile grid shows.

## Schedule

Saudi work week is Sunday to Thursday. Instagram posts go out in the evening and LinkedIn in the morning. Treat these times as starting points and adjust them once your Insights show when your audience is online. During Ramadan, move Instagram to about 10:30 pm, after tarawih.

| | Sun | Wed |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 (launch)** | **Launch drop, 8:30 pm:** IG 1, IG 2 and IG 3 posted together, so visitors from LinkedIn find a full first row. **LI 1 at 9:30 am.** | IG 4 reel |
| **Week 2** | IG 5 carousel · **LI 2 (Mon or Tue 9:30 am)** | IG 6 image |
| **Week 3** | IG 7 reel · **LI 3 (Mon or Tue 9:30 am)** | IG 8 carousel |
| **Week 4** | IG 9 image | |

Thursday evening is the start of the weekend, which is weak for a B2B audience, so the second weekly post goes on Wednesday.

**Before posting:**
- **Reel covers:** upload the matching `*cover.png` as each reel's custom cover so the grid stays consistent.
- **Link in bio:** Instagram captions can't hold clickable links, so put fleetana.sa in your bio and say "link in bio".
- **Don't pin posts during the first 9.** Pinning moves a post to the top-left and breaks the column layout. Once the grid is complete, pinning IG 2 ("Your whole fleet. One place.") works well as the introduction.
- **LinkedIn:** a brand-new company page gets very little reach. Have the founder post LI 1 from their personal profile, and have the company page reshare it.

## Where each claim comes from in the code
- **Fuel flag:** below 70% of the 30-day average (`lib/fuel-economy.ts`, `ANOMALY_THRESHOLD = 0.7`). Economy is the distance since the last full fill ÷ the fuel added.
- **Service reminders:** due by meter (km or hours) or by date, whichever comes first, with Upcoming / Due soon / Overdue statuses (`lib/service-reminders.ts`).
- **Expiries:** listed within 30 days on the dashboard (`app/(app)/dashboard`). This is on screen only. Push notifications go out only for urgent issues and work orders awaiting approval (`lib/push.ts`), so no post says "alert" or "notice" for fuel, reminders or expiries.
- **Failed inspection:** a failed item creates an issue (`lib/actions/inspections.ts`).
- **Work orders:** parts, labour and fee lines with 15% VAT (`lib/work-orders.ts`, `prisma/schema.prisma`).
- **Driver score:** `100 − (harsh events/100 km × 2 + overspeed/100 km × 4 + collisions × 30)`. Drivers under 25 km aren't rated (`lib/telematics/driver-score.ts`).
- **Live map, idle time and driver scores** all require a GT06 tracker (`services/tracker-ingest`).
