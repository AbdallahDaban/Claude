# Fleetana Logo (v2)

Source files: `public/fleetana-logo.svg` (lockup) and `public/fleetana-mark.svg` (the chevron F). Copies are in `assets/`, alongside PNGs.

## Anatomy
The lockup reads FLEETANA: the **chevron F** (two stacked chevron shapes pointing right) followed by the drawn letters **L E E T A N A**. The F *is* the mark. That's why the wordmark on its own reads "LEETANA", so never separate the letters from the mark.

- Ratio about 7.6 : 1, horizontal only.
- **Mark** (the chevron F alone): use it as the icon, favicon, avatar and app icon, and wherever the lockup would be under 120 px wide.

## Color
| Background | Logo |
|---|---|
| White, Paper, Tint, light photos | Fleetana Blue `#2A89FA` (default) |
| Fleetana Blue fill or a dark photo | White |
| One-color print, fax, stamps | Black |

Use no other colors, and no gradients, outlines, shadows or glows.

## Size and space
- Minimum lockup width: 120 px on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, use the mark.
- Clear space on every side equals the height of the chevron F.
- Placement:
  - Social posts: 30 px tall at top-left (top-right in RTL), 80 px from the edges of a 1080 canvas.
  - Slides: 0.28–0.32 in tall.
  - Documents: 10 mm tall in the header.

## Arabic
- **Never mirror the logo.** Change its position instead: top-right in RTL layouts.
- Until a drawn Arabic wordmark exists, set فليتانا in IBM Plex Sans Arabic 600 under or beside the lockup at about 45% of the lockup height. Never type it in a stock font as a replacement for the lockup.
- Open item: commission a drawn Arabic wordmark that matches the chevron and stroke weight. Use the transliteration فليتانا, not أسطولنا.

## Motion
The logo builds chevron-first:
1. The two chevrons slide in from the left with the house ease, upper then lower, 0.1–0.2 s apart.
2. The letters rise into place behind a mask, about 0.06 s apart.
3. The finished lockup holds for at least 2.5 s.

At the end of a film the lockup may sit on a soft radial glow of Fleetana Blue at 14–18%.

## Never
Retype it, stretch it, rotate it, mirror it, recolor it, add effects, place it on busy photos without a clear area, or separate LEETANA from the F.
